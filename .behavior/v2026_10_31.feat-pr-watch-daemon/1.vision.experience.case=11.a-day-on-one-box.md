## .narrative

09:00 — two mechanics on v1.41.0, `beav.feat-a` and `beav.feat-b`, push in the same second. no daemon lives. one findsert spawns pid 48122; the other waits on the lock and joins it. one daemon, two watches (#1220, #1221).

09:07 — `test-unit` fails on #1220. clone A is mid-turn on its next stone. `clone say` refuses; the alert is held. at 09:09 clone A idles; the held alert lands, marked `held 2 ticks`.

09:15 — clone A pushes a fix. the head sha changes; the check cursor moves to the new sha. the old failure does not re-alert against the new run.

10:30 — a reviewer leaves a review with two inline comments on #1221. clone B gets one grouped alert. it replies via `git.pull.comment set`. its own 🐢 reply raises no alert.

18:10 — the token expires. both clones get one `pr watch is paused` alert, with the unlock command. clone A runs the unlock at 18:14. the daemon resumes from each cursor; a comment that landed at 18:12 is alerted once.

18:30 — the commander approves #1220. clone A gets the approval alert with `ci: passed` and runs `git.release`. #1220 merges; clone A gets `watch ended`.

19:05 — #1221 merges. that was the last watch. the daemon removes its pid record and exits. the box holds zero pr-watch processes.

## .where it is most likely to break

- t1 × t2: the fix push changes the head sha mid-watch. the check cursor must key on sha, or the old failure re-alerts against the new run
- t5 × t3: a comment that lands while paused must be alerted once at resume, not lost and not twice
- t6 × t7: the merge of #1220 must not reap the daemon while #1221 still watches
