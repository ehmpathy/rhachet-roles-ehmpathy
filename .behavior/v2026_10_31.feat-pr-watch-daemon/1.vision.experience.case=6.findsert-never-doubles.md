# case=6 · the findsert never doubles

covers: L18 (race × absent), L21 (restart × absent), L22 (restart × live), F1. sharp · critipath.

## .narrative

two mechanics on v1.41.0 push in the same second. no daemon lives. both reach the findsert.

each takes the version's lock before it reads the pid record. clone A wins the lock, sees no live pid, spawns pid 48122, writes the record, and releases. clone B waits on the lock, then reads the record, finds pid 48122 alive, and joins it.

later clone A re-runs its push after a rebase. its findsert takes the lock, finds 48122 alive, and joins. still one daemon.

the daemon also guards itself: at boot it takes the same lock and checks the pid record. if a live daemon of its version is already on record, it exits at once. so even a spawn that slips past the caller's check cannot leave two alive.
