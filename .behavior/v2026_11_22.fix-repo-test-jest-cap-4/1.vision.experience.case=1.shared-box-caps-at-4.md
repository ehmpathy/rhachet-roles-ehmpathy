# case=1: a shared box caps jest at 4

cell #1 · shared × none × above-cap × command none · happy · critipath · densest by system boundaries (case=5 is densest by decision boundaries)

## .narrative — the invoker

three crews work on one 36-core grove. each repo's jest config says `maxWorkers: '50%'`, so today each crew's jest asks for 18 workers. two crews' suites fill the box with 36 workers. the third crew's suite lands on a full box. all three run at the same `nice 5`, so nice ranks none above the others: 54 workers share 36 cores, and a fourth crew's suite (also at nice 5) finds no free core at all. only work at nice 0, such as a build, still cuts ahead.

with the cap, crew one runs `rhx git.repo.test --what integration --mode apply`. the skill sees no `CI`, asks jest what count it would use (18), and passes `--maxWorkers=4`. crews two and three do the same. three suites run 12 workers on 36 cores. each suite is a bit slower than its share of a full box would be, and in exchange 24 cores stay free: a fourth crew's suite starts at once.

crew one notices naught different in the output. the `--help` line says why the run uses 4 workers and how to ask for more.

## .narrative — the neighbor

i am crew four. i never call anyone else's skill and i cannot see their runs. i just pushed a fix and want my integration suite green before i open the pr.

before the cap, i kick off `rhx git.repo.test --what integration --mode apply` and the `💤 inflight` lines stack well past what the suite takes on a quiet box. `top` shows three other jest pools at nice 5, 54 workers deep, and my pool asks for 18 more on a box with no core free. i wait, or i kill it and come back later.

with the cap, the same three crews hold 12 workers between them. my suite gets its 4 workers at once and lands in about the time it takes on a quiet box with 4 workers. i never learn the other crews were there. that is the point.

## .bdd sketch

```
given a shared box (CI unset) with 36 cores
  and a jest config with maxWorkers: '50%'  (jest derives 18)
  when [t0] a crew runs `rhx git.repo.test --what integration --mode apply`
    then jest receives `--maxWorkers=4`
    then the suite runs on 4 workers
    then stdout matches today's shape (no new line)
  when [t1] two more crews run their suites at once
    then the box holds 12 jest workers, not 54
  when [t2] a fourth crew (the neighbor) runs `rhx git.repo.test --what integration --mode apply`
    then its 4 workers start at once on free cores
    then its suite time matches a quiet box with 4 workers
    then it needs no knowledge of the other crews
```
