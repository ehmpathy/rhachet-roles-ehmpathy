## .narrative

at 9am: an upgraded tree, `feat/swell-alerts`, zero commits ahead and twelve behind main. its old
meter file still holds `"push": "allow"` and `"stage": "block"`. the human grants one commit; the
render notes the retired fields and that release now needs its own stamp. the crew fast-forwards
onto main on the commit grant alone, stages, and commits — the last use. it pushes anyway: the grant
is held, not revoked. a second commit refuses. the crew tries to ship: refused, by meter. the human
stamps one release; the crew ships with zero commit uses left. last, the human types `block` on the
commit meter: push stops, the release meter reads as before.
