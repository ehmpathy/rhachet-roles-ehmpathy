## .narrative

at 9am: an upgraded tree, `feat/swell-alerts`, zero commits ahead and twelve behind main. its old
meter file still holds `"push": "allow"`. the human grants one commit; the render warns that the old
push grant was dropped and names how to regrant it. the crew fast-forwards onto main on the commit
grant alone. it writes the fix, then runs `git.commit.set --push` out of habit — that refuses before
any commit lands. it commits without `--push`; the commit spends the last use. the human grants
push; the crew pushes; push did not vanish with the depleted commit quota. the crew tries to ship:
refused. the human stamps one release; the crew ships with zero commits left. last, the human
revokes push; the commit and release meters read exactly as before.
