## divergences from the vision

- the fault is named by kind (`ORG_KEYRACK_FAULT`, `ORG_UNREADABLE_KIND`), not a bool: each kind takes its own note and its own preview lift; a bool could carry neither.
- the gate's org-name set is narrower than keyrack's (a plain name: letters, digits, `_ . -`); a name outside it refuses closed and the note states the rule (F12).
- forms keyrack reads that the gate refuses closed: a flow list across lines, a yaml anchor, a quoted key, a name with a space or other punctuation. each refusal shows the line read.
- lint `depcheck` reports three unused devDependencies. they come from the worktree's uncommitted `package.json` bump, not from this change, and do not ship in this pr.
