## .narrative

beaver's merge stops on eleven conflicts across a module main rewrote. that is no catch-up; it is a
redesign. beaver backs out. `abort` restores HEAD, index, and tree to the moment before `begin`,
spends no meter, and says so. beaver escalates the redesign question instead of a guess at eleven
files. the exit is clean, so the next attempt starts from a known state.
