**why no-quota and dirty-tree are critipaths and the other gates alterpaths** (care = freq × cost):

- no-quota: **frequent** — every grant is a finite N and a crew spends it daily; the meter is built
  to run dry. **costly** — the merge is the act that unblocks a stuck pr. high × high
- dirty-tree: **frequent** — a clone mid-task often holds edits when it decides to catch up.
  **costly** — a merge over them would hide work in a commit no one reviews. high × high
- blocked-global-or-org: **rare** — a human sets it on purpose to halt all commits; every commit
  verb refuses alike. low × low
- no-sponsor: **rare** for this verb — `git.commit.set` refuses a sponsorless clone on its first
  commit, long before it has a pr to catch up. low × medium
- rebase-in-flight, fetch-fails: **rare** — a clone mixes verbs mid-flight, or the network drops.
  low × low
