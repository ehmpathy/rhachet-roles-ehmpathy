# case=1 · a merged or closed pr ends its watch, and no session brings the daemon back

covers: E1 (merged), E1c (closed without merge). happy · critipath.

## .narrative

clone @:mechanic watches two prs of ehmpathy/surf-demo: #1220 and #1222.

#1220 merges at 14h02. the next tick reads `merged`, sends one note, and deletes the record once the note is out:

```
🐢 cowabunga!

🐚 git.pull.watch — #1220 merged; watch ended
```

a human closes #1222 without merge at 14h10. the next tick reads `closed`, and the note names who closed it, so a close never reads as a ship:

```
🐢 bummer dude...

🐚 git.pull.watch — #1222 closed without merge; watch ended
   └─ by: @casey-surf
```

(both notes are the extant `as_watch_closure_text`, `git.pull.watch.alert.sh:217`; the `by:` login comes from the one read of `issues/$n` that a closed pr earns, `git.pull.watch.github.sh:269`)

the record leaves once that note is out. the daemon holds zero watches and exits.

at 14h30 the clone starts a new session. the SessionStart hook counts the records a daemon of its key would serve: zero. it spawns naught and prints naught. no `bash`, no `rhachet`, no `gh` is born for #1220 or #1222 again.

merged and closed share one end path: the diff sets `prEnd` (`git.pull.watch.daemon.sh:201`), the record is marked `ended`, the drain deletes it once its outbox is empty (`:653`–`662`). they differ only in the note.
