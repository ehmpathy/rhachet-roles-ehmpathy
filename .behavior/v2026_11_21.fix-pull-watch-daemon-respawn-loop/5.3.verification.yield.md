- `git.pull.watch.lifecycle [case9b][t2]` (wedged): the scene's `stopAllDaemons` sent SIGKILL to the daemon pid alone, so a child it forked mid-tick outlived it and wrote a fresh heartbeat over the test's stale one. fixed at the cause: the daemon leads its own session (`setsid`), so the scene now kills the whole group (`process.kill(-pid)`), with the bare pid as the fallback. the suite then passed: `103 passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped`. the same pass taught the scene's `isKeyLive` to check the pid is alive, not just that its record exists (a killed daemon leaves the record behind). every pull-watch suite then passed again (row above)
