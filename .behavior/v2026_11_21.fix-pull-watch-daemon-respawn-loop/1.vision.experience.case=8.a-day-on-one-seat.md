# case=8 · a day on one seat: upgrade, a 404, an hourly token, a keyrack lock, a merge

covers: E1, E2, E4, E5, E6, E8, E10, E13, E26 in one walk. composite · critipath: a journey with no feel of its own; each experience it crosses keeps its own row's feel in the inventory. the densest demo: it crosses the steal, the 404 end, the held token and its swap to the grove token, the keyrack lock, the merge end, and the hook's spawn judgment.

## .narrative

monday 09h00. seat @:mechanic runs v1.42.0 and watches two prs: #1230 (live) and #1231 (its sandbox repo was just deleted, so it now answers 404). a human unlocks keyrack.

monday 09h30. the seat upgrades to v1.43.0 and starts a session. the hook counts two servable records under v1.42.0 (both live, root runs v1.43.0) and spawns a v1.43.0 daemon. its first tick steals both. the old daemon holds zero watches and exits.

the first due read needs a token: one keyrack read, seaturtle granted, held. #1231 answers 404. the clone hears `#1231 answers 404 (gone, or this token cannot see it); watch ended`, with the rewatch command, and the record leaves.

monday 10h00. the seaturtle token dies at its hour. the next due read of #1230 gets a 401; one re-read finds the seaturtle key purged and grants the grove token; the retry reads fine. no note.

monday 10h00 to tuesday. #1230 grows quiet; its gap stretches to 30 minutes. the daemon holds the grove token and reads keyrack no more. keyrack's own unlock lapses at 18h00, but github still accepts the held grove token, so the daemon does not pause (F1 names this tradeoff).

tuesday 07h50. #1230 merges. the next due read sends `#1230 merged; watch ended`. the daemon holds zero watches and exits. over 22 hours it made two keyrack reads, where today it would make more than 2,500.

tuesday 09h00. the clone starts a session. the hook finds no servable record under any key and spawns naught.
