# case 13 — amend-misses-a-copy · sharp critipath (fails loud)

cell: family G · `min-twin × drifts` and `role-twin × drifts` · forbidden invariant: an amend reaches every copy a clone boots

## .narrative

I10 amends `rule.require.clamp-edge-cases` (mechanic): a clamp names the invariant it guards. the clone writes the line into the full `.md`. the brief boots `say`, so the reach check passes.

but a `say` brief with a `.min` twin boots as the twin. the clone never reads the full `.md` at boot; the amend never reaches it. `boot.lessons.integration.test.ts` `[t1]` looks up the amend line in the boot payload a consumer receives, and fails red until the `.min` carries it too.

the same brief text can also live in two roles. `define.invariant` ships in the architect (`domain.constraints/`) and the mechanic (`lang.terms/`). an author who sharpens one copy leaves the other role to boot the old text, in silence. `briefs.twins.integration.test.ts` reads both copies of each marked twin and asserts one shared text, apart from each copy's own citations; and that each citation reaches a brief its role ships, or names the role that does.

## .bdd `[tn]` timeline

```
given rule.require.clamp-edge-cases boots say, as its .min twin
  when [t0] the clone writes the I10 line into the full .md only
    then boot.lessons [t0] passes (the reach is say)
    and  boot.lessons [t1] fails red: the line is absent from the boot payload
  when [t1] the clone carries the line into the .min
    then boot.lessons [t1] passes
given define.invariant ships in the architect and the mechanic
  when [t2] the clone adds a line to the mechanic copy only
    then briefs.twins fails red: "they hold one shared text, apart from their own citations"
  when [t3] the clone copies the line into the architect twin
    then briefs.twins passes
```

## .proof

real tests, not a sketch:

- `[t0]`, `[t1]`: `src/domain.roles/boot.lessons.integration.test.ts` `[t1]`, `AMENDS_BOOTED`. bite proven in 5.3: the line cut from the `.min` → 8 passed, 1 failed; restored → 9 passed
- `[t2]`, `[t3]`: `src/domain.roles/briefs.twins.integration.test.ts`. bite proven in 5.3: `- a board is lent to one surfer at a time` added to the mechanic copy only → 16 passed, 1 failed (`the define.invariant.md twin › … they hold one shared text`); removed → 17 passed

## .why critipath

every amend in this route lands on a brief that has a `.min` twin or a role twin (freq: each amend). a copy left stale boots the old rule in one role or one tier with no signal (cost: a lesson the wisher ruled in, never read).
