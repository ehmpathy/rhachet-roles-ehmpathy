but the pair's two poles have swapped roles. the forbid pole names the smell a clone can see at the keyboard (a read, then a write on its result) and carries the cure. its require twin names a target, easy to drift past. with the forbid at `ref`, a reviewer clone that boots the architect never reads the ban before it reviews a double-book.

the same check holds the other direction: a require twin moved up to `say`, or a catalog member booted at all (`[t2]`: the 13 transaction members boot at no reach), fails red on the brief.
