## .quote

> "A Greek philosopher of Ephesus ... who was active around 500 BCE ... He is best known for his doctrines that things are constantly changing (universal flux)"

> "Heraclitus, I believe, says that all things pass and nothing stays, and comparing existing things to the flow of a river, he says you could not step twice into the same river." (Plato, Cratylus 402a = A6, as quoted in the entry)

## .supports

- the flux pole: change is the default state of the world; an invariant is what a mind holds fixed against it
- caveat: the entry notes scholars dispute Plato's strong reading of the flux doctrine
