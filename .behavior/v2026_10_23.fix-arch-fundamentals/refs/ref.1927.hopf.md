## .quote

> "Solutions of a partial differential equation (or, more generally, of a differential inequality) in a domain D are said to satisfy the maximum principle if they achieve their maxima at the boundary of D. Harmonic functions and, more generally, solutions of elliptic partial differential equations satisfy the maximum principle." (Wikipedia, *Maximum principle*)

> "The maximum principle enables one to obtain information about solutions of differential equations without any explicit knowledge of the solutions themselves." (same)
