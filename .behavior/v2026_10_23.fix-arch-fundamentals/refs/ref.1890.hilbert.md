> "Hilbert's first work was on invariant theory and, in 1888, he proved his famous Basis Theorem. ... He discovered a completely new approach which proved the finite basis theorem for any number of variables but in an entirely abstract way. Although he proved that a finite basis existed his methods did not construct such a basis." (MacTutor)
