## .quote

> "The term INVARIANT was coined by James Joseph Sylvester. It appears in 1851 in 'On A Remarkable Discovery in the Theory of Canonical Forms and of Hyperdeterminants,' Philosophical Magazine, 4th Ser., 2, 391-410: 'The remaining coefficients are the two well-known hyperdeterminants, or, as I propose henceforth to call them, the two Invariants of the form ax4 + 4bx3y + 6cx2y2 + 4dxy3 + ey4.'"

> "If I (a, b,..l) = I (a', b',..l'), then I is defined to be an invariant of f."

## .supports

- the word "invariant" dates to 1851, and its first definition is a relation that holds before and after a transformation
