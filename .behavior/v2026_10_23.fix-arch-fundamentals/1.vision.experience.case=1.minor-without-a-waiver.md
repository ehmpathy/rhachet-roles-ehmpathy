# case 1 — minor-without-a-waiver · sharp critipath (fails safe)

cell: family A · `build × value × once × unstated` · lessons I01, I02, I04, I17 · tactics A02, A09, A14 (ours O01, O07, O15)

## .narrative

the surf school adds online signup and gift vouchers. a mechanic clone reads `birthDate` off the surfer row and writes a check in `setLessonReservation`: under 18 with no waiver → refuse. two defects ride that one line. the birth date — pii — now flows through every operation that touches a surfer. and a later change adds voucher redemption, which writes a reservation through the dao directly and skips the check. a 15-year-old paddles out with no parent waiver on file.

with I01 booted, the clone asks first: **which constraint, and how is it enforced?** it states the invariant on the object it governs, in the domain's own concept. the rule is not about a birth date; it is about consent:

> **every lesson reservation carries the consent its surfer requires on the lesson date: the surfer's own, or a parent's.**

four choices follow from that line:

- **the pii stays in the dao** (I17). the dao masks the birth date into the concept the domain needs: `daoSurfer.getConsentRequired({ surfer, on: lessonDate })` returns `SELF` or `PARENT`. no domain object carries a birth date or an age, and no surfer type forks on age
- **the reservation enforces the invariant, on every path.** the `LessonReservation` domain object refuses construction when its consent does not match the consent required — a `ConstraintError` that names the fix (*"this surfer needs a parent's consent; send the parent the waiver link"*)
- **one write path.** `setLessonReservation` is the one operation that writes a reservation: signup, voucher, and any future path call it, never the dao

- **raw pii leaves only through the quarantine.** the fix the error names — send the parent the waiver link — needs the parent's email. `sendParentWaiverLink` is the one path that reads it: authorized, audited without the value, handed straight to the email provider, no pii returned to its caller (I17, I20)

one surfer shape, one reservation shape, one write path, and the pii in one quarantine.

## .why critipath

every repo we ship carries rules of this shape: an act that is legal only when some other fact is true (freq high). when the rule lives in one caller instead of on the object it governs, the next caller forgets it, and the defect is silent until harm lands — here a safety and legal harm, plus pii spread across the domain (cost high). no workable fallback exists once it ships: a check in one path fails only on the path that lacks it.

## .what it teaches about abstraction

- the invariant belongs on the aggregate it governs (A14), with its contract stated (A09) and one write path to it
- the dao is where a raw fact becomes a domain concept (A02, O15, I17): a birth date becomes a consent requirement. pii never crosses into the domain, and the domain speaks in the words the rule needs
- *make illegal states unrepresentable* (A11) applies to a **stable** fact a type owns: money is never a float (`IsoPrice`). it does not license a type per value of a derived or time-varied fact; a split on age forks every path and goes stale on a birthday
