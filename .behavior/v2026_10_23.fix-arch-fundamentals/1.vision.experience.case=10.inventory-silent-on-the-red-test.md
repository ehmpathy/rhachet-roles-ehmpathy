# case 10 — inventory-silent-on-the-red-test · sharp critipath (fails safe, self-sufficient)

cell: family D · `build × unnamed × derives` · lessons I27, I28, I25

## .narrative

a robot mechanic adds lesson cancellation. a test goes red: `refuses a reservation for a cancelled lesson` now passes the reservation through. the test cites no entry, and the inventory holds none on cancellation.

the cheap move: delete the test as stale. I28 bites: no name, no edit. and a halt to ask a human is no better: it stops the work on a question the inventory may answer.

first, is it glue? no: it pins no call, shape, or order of this implementation; it asserts what the domain refuses, whatever the code. so it reads as a domain rule no entry names, and the robot derives.

it skepticizes the omission in order. an implicit invariant? the instructor's words, kept in `domain.terms/lesson-cancellation.md`, say a cancelled lesson "has no slots left to fill". an implicit assumption? none — the store holds the lesson status. a deduction from the entries on record? yes: `i001` says reserved boards stay within stock, and cancellation sets the slot's stock to zero. a reservation holds a board. so no reservation can stand on a cancelled lesson.

the robot records `i004-cancelled-lesson-holds-no-reservations`; its `relies on` field cites `i001`, and its `.verified` field reads "best guess", itemized as a fulcrum for a human to verify. the test now cites `[i004]`. the robot fixes the cancellation path: it sets stock to zero through `setLessonSlot`, which the version check already guards. the test goes green unchanged.

had the analysis found no clear answer, the robot would still not halt: it records the likeliest entry as a best guess, itemizes the fulcrum, and acts. if the human overturns the guess, the reflection asks which entry was absent, and why no derivation could reach it.

## .why critipath

most tests cite no entry and need none: they are glue. but a test that asserts a domain rule with no cited entry is common in any repo whose inventory is young (freq high). a delete there loses a domain rule no one wrote down; a halt there spends the scarcest resource on a question the inventory could answer, and stalls the work besides (cost: behavioral; human time). a recorded guess costs the human one glance at the fulcrum council. the derivation turns each silence into one more lit corner.
