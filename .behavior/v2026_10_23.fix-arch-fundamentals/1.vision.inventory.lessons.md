## .the spine

one idea runs through every row: **a constraint shrinks the space the system can reach, and every constraint has an enforcement.** three constraints: an **invariant** (we keep it, inside), an **assumption** (the world keeps it; we hold evidence), a **guarantee** (we keep it, for consumers at a release boundary). a **structural** enforcement leaves no path to the break — by compilation (a type, a closed enum) or by abstraction (a domain object that refuses construction, a store constraint, one write path). an **operational** enforcement catches the break — proactive at test time (a test, induction), reactive at runtime (an assertion in the path), or reactive by monitor. a guard in one caller is neither. the declaration says what must be true; the enforcement is the implementer's choice of how.
