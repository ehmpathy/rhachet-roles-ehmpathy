## .why critipath

the wisher's own words predict this shape: *"i expect to disagree with some of the choices"*. some, not most (freq: every review of this route).

case 6's halt is a fallback per row, but not a workable route to the goal. the goal is a real review of the calls the wisher disputes. at ~86 rows across the three inventories, the halt fires per blank row, or the wisher types ~86 `agree` cells to silence it. either way the cost lands on the rows the wisher did **not** dispute, and the cheap escape is a skim that types `agree` down the column (cost: the review rubber-stamps the very calls it exists to check). a route that reaches "every row ruled" by way of a rubber stamp misses the goal, so it does not count as a fallback, and the care is critipath.
