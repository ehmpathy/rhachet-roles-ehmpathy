### ⚠️ what the credit does NOT count — a cell NO `[tn]` walks

peer review r7 asked why cells `69/70` are absent from the credit, since they are now `criti` and are
behaviorally identical to the demoed `45/46`. ⇒ **because no `[tn]` walks them.** their note

| the row | may carry a demo credit? |
|---|---|
| an `[tn]` walks this cell | ✅ **yes**, whatever the verdict — `demoed` and `forbidden` alike |

the credit: a cell an `[tn]` walks is `demoed` (or `forbidden`-and-demoed), and a cell none walks is

rather than about the row's evidence.** the repair is to anchor the credit in the checkable fact

## 🔴 .the boundary-density order

**density measures the DEMO, never the cell.** a boundary is an edge where the system's response
changes — a rejection, a limit, a seam — and density counts how many of them one walk exercises
(`define.experience._.metric=boundary-density`).

⚠️ this section once ranked cells by *how many axes a cell sits at an extreme of*. that is a
property of a **cell**, and density is an evaluation lens on a **demo**. the misread is corrected
here, and it cost a real gap — see below.

|---|---|---|
| **1** | **`case=7`** — the first day of a cloud tree | **6** — t0 reject · t1 actor · t2 identity · t3 bind · t4 commit · t5 seam |
| 2 | `case=4` — clone cannot sponsor itself | 2 — the actor refusal, plus the permitted read |
| 3 | `case=5` — bind refuses a robot | 2 — the robot refusal, plus the placeholder refusal |
| 4 | **`case=8`** — the dispatcher stall | **2** — the commit refusal, plus the refused bind that follows it |

| 8 | `case=2` — the commit names a human | 1 — the happy commit |

## 🔴 .the C=`unreadable` block — 24 cells added at peer review i002
