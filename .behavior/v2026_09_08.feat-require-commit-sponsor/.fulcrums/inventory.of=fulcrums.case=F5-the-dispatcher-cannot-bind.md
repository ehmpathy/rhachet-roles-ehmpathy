## .the fork, stated fairly

the actor axis has two values: `human` and `clone`. the guard refuses `clone`.

but `clone` holds two different parties:

| party | what it is | does the guard fit? |
|---|---|---|
| a **mechanic** at work on a tree | the party that must never sponsor itself | ✅ refuse. this is `case=4` |
| a **dispatcher** that provisions cloud trees | acts under a human's **delegated authority**, granted before the tree existed | ❌ refuse, and automated dispatch stalls at its first commit |

⇒ a latent fifth axis: **`has-delegated-authority?`** (`1.vision.experience.dimensions.md` → the
latent axis).

## .taken, and why at the time

**refuse both. do not resolve the dispatcher case in this vision.**

two reasons:

1. **strict is the reversible direction.** a guard that refuses too much blocks a workflow, loudly,
   and can be loosened. a guard that permits a dispatcher wrongly reopens cell 12 — the defect —
   and the loosened form is what ships.
2. 🔴 **the answer is the wisher's, not ours.** to let a dispatcher bind is to say a **delegated,
   non-human party may originate an authorization.** that is a claim about where accountability
   originates — the exact claim `#645` builds the whole wish on. we do not get to weaken it by a
   design convenience.

⇒ 🔴 **the real dirt is PROVENANCE.** the design's guards observe a tty and a permission list;
neither can answer *"did a human grant this party authority before the tree existed?"* to admit a
dispatcher is to add a **new kind of evidence** the system does not carry, and every later guard
would lean on it.

## 🔴 what the walk PRICED — the cost of the current call is now demonstrated

the walk produced a **critipath** this row had only asserted: `dispatcher × commit × none` —
[`case=8`](../1.vision.experience.case=8.a-dispatcher-provisioned-tree-stalls.md).

⇒ **automated dispatch stalls at its first commit, and the refusal names a remedy no party present
can perform** (*"ask your human, at a tty"* — there is no human and no tty). the stall is loud and
recoverable, which is the one mercy; recovery still costs a human an ssh session per tree.

⚠️ and `case=8` `[t1]` shows why a guard cannot simply be taught the difference: a dispatcher that
offers a real human's name from a queued task is **byte-identical at the command** to `case=4`'s
fabrication. the acts differ only in provenance.

⇒ **what changes for the wisher:** the ask is no longer *"should we support dispatchers?"* but
🔴 *"automated dispatch stalls at its first commit — is that acceptable, or must it be bought
back?"* **a cost demonstrated beats a question deferred.**
