## 🔴 .the rule this case establishes — a REFUSAL is byte-copied, a SUCCESS TREE is shape-copied

the family holds both kinds, and they owe different fidelity because the **contract lives in a
different place**:

| the render | what IS the contract | what a vignette owes |
|---|---|---|
| a **refusal** (`case=3`, `case=4`, `case=5`) | 🔴 **the words.** the body text, the `⛔` line, which commands print | **byte-for-byte.** a paraphrase teaches a refusal that never shipped |
| a **success tree** (`case=2`, `case=1` `[t1]`) | 🔴 **the shape.** leaf order, labels, which block holds which field | **shape-for-shape**, with narrative values |

⇒ 🎯 **the test: could a reader ACT on the difference?** a human reads a refusal and copies its
command, so a wrong word is a wrong act. nobody copies `fix(test): plan test` out of a vision doc —
they read the tree to learn *where the sponsor block sits and what it is called*.

⇒ ⚠️ **and a literal copy here would be WORSE**, not merely equal: a narrative in which
`Test Human` authorizes `fix(test): plan test` teaches the mechanism with the domain stripped out,
and this case is the one the whole wish exists for (`rule.forbid.snapshot-visual-blemishes` targets
a render that **misleads**, never one that illustrates).

⇒ the `then:` rows below state the shape claim explicitly and name the pin they check it against.

## .the narrative

the mechanic finishes a fix and commits. it does not read `git config`. it does not ask who the
human is. it reads the sponsor that was bound before the tree came up, and writes that human into
the trailer.

the tree output names two parties and says which is which: the **author** is the robot that wrote
the code, the **sponsor** is the human who authorized the work. the commit that lands says the
same, in git's own vocabulary.

⇒ **the aha:** the grove no longer decides the answer. a cloud tree and a local tree produce the
identical trailer, because both read the same bound value. the axis that carried the defect
(`1.vision.experience.dimensions.md` → D) goes inert here entirely.

## .what makes it a critipath

| term | value |
|---|---|
| frequency | every commit, every tree, forever |
| cost if it breaks | a commit with no accountable human reaches prod, and it *looks* attributed |
| workable fallback | **none** — a hand-supplied trailer is refused, and a wrong one is a fabrication |

⇒ `freq × cost` ∞. **critipath.**

## 🔴 what this case must NOT do

the seed issue `#646` frames the repair as *"the adhoc `Co-authored-by` guard must learn to tell a
bound sponsor from an invented one."*

⇒ **this design gives that guard no new lesson, and that is deliberate.**

| route to a trailer | verdict |
|---|---|
| derived from `git config` | **removed** — the defect |
| supplied in the message by hand | **still refused** — the guard stays exactly as it is |
| read from the bound sponsor | **new, and the only route** |

the sponsor never travels in the message, so the guard never has to sort authorization from
invention. it keeps its one job: refuse an invented trailer.

⇒ `#646` says the two extant routes close every path. the repair opens a **third** route rather
than loosens either — a smaller change with a strictly smaller blast radius, and it leaves the
invention guard at full strength.

## .the ubiqlang caveat

the **label** reads `sponsor`, not `patron` — at both sites in `git.commit.set.sh`:
`echo "   │  ├─ sponsor"` in the plan tree, `echo "   │  └─ sponsor"` in the apply tree.

⚠️ **the two are not one literal, and an earlier draft here said they were** — it quoted the `├─`
form and claimed it renders "twice". the glyph differs because the trees differ in **shape**: in
the plan tree a `files` block follows `sponsor`, so it is mid-list (`├─`); in the apply tree it is
the last leaf under `commit`, so it closes the group (`└─`). ⇒ a single quoted literal cannot stand
as proof for both sites, and the label — never the line — is what this caveat is about.

⇒ the rename is of a **rendered label**, so it moves the snapshot baseline. the word is a
fulcrum — `F1`, and the evidence is at
`.agent/repo=.this/role=any/briefs/domain.terms/sponsor.md`.
