# case=3 — a novel command, no paved alternative

demos inventory #3 (`single · {novel}`).

## .narrative

a mechanic wants the count of snapshot entries per suite and reaches for
`python3 -c "import json,glob; …"`. `python3` has no inventory entry, and no allowlist pattern
shares its head or two of its tokens.

the nudge says so plainly — *"no paved path fits"* — and does not pretend a weak fuzzy hit is an
answer. it tells the mechanic the right move: pave a reusable rhx skill (or ask its human to), and
only retry the adhoc form if the need is truly one-off. the event lands in the ledger as `novel`,
so the demand for a paved path is visible later.
