## what

add a `PermissionRequest` brain-hook event to the rhachet role contract — a peer of `onTool`, or
an `onTool` filter value — so a role can declare a PermissionRequest hook and `roles boot`
generates its `.claude/settings.json` entry.

## why

`ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy` ships `permissionrequest.decide-permissions.sh`, which
auto-decides suspicious-classified permission prompts. the contract today holds only `onBoot`,
`onTool` (PreToolUse), and `onStop`, so the hook is registered by a hand-written settings.json
entry (#576). that entry has no role source: a stale settings.json can carry a broken path for
it with no role-level check to catch it, and a hook that never runs retires its gate in silence.

## shape

1. add the event to the brain-hook contract and the claude-code adapter (`PermissionRequest`,
   with a matcher such as `Bash`)
2. the mechanic role then declares it via `asHookCommand('permissionrequest.decide-permissions')`
   beside its other hooks
3. the role repo retires the hand-written entry — verify byte-identical first, so consumer
   repos see no churn

## evidence

`.agent/repo=.this/role=user/briefs/brains/howto.use.brain.hooks.md:10-16,96-103` lists the three
events the contract holds today.
