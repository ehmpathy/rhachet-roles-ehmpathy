# case=5 — a raw `pnpm patch` is redirected to the paved verb, with no human pulled

feel = sharp → happy · care = critipath (the defect the wish names) · covers c16, c18

## .narrative

a clone on the crew — a bot, with no hand to click a modal — types the command it knows:
`pnpm patch rhachet@1.48.1`. last week that exact line left it parked until a director clicked
through a classifier prompt. this time a reply comes back in under a second: not paved, and here
is the verb to use instead, `rhx git.package.patch begin --package <name>`. the clone retypes it
that way. no prompt appears; the edit dir opens. after its edit, out of habit it types
`pnpm patch-commit <dir>` — and gets the same fast reply, this time with the `commit` line,
`--why` and `--upstream` in place. it fills them in and moves on. no human was pulled.
