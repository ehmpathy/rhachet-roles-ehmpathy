# case=5 · a review burst beside a red check, one thread answered via relay

covers: M2, M3, C1, C2, C9, S2, plus the director. happy · critipath. the densest walk: five boundaries in one read.

## .narrative

the commander reviews in bursts. on #1222 he opened 6 threads in 5 minutes, one at a time; on #1143 he drafted 8 and submitted them at one instant, so one read saw all 8.

on `#7` he submits a review with three inline threads. a minute before, `test-unit` went red on the crew's last push. a lint bot posted a fourth inline comment. the director, who reads the pr, relayed one thread — "--pr -> --which plz" — and the crew answered it at once.

the daemon's next read sees all of it. it sorts the moves:

- the bot's comment → naught (case=3)
- the relayed thread → answered by the crew, ledgered → naught (case=2)
- two unanswered threads → a reply task each
- the red check → a `ci failed` section, as today

all of one read rides one alert, as today (A9). the reply tasks lead it, and the header names them first. the crew is mid-turn on the red check, so `clone say` enqueues it:
