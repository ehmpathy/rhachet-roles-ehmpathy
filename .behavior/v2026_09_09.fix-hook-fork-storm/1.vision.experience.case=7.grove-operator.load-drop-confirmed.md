# case=7 — the operator confirms the load drop

**actor** grove-operator · **feel** happy · **care** critipath
**cell** outside the gate box — the void row that surfaced the `gated?` axis

the only actor who never passes through the gate, and the only one who can confirm the wish was
granted. their evidence is a sum over ~17 clones, never a single edit.

## .the narrative

the beaver watches `grove-ahbode-v20260901` — 4 cpu, 31G ram, ~17 clones. the roll-up that filed
this wish read:

```
  71.4% cpu · n=8    run.bun.rhachet-run.bc
  63.2% cpu · n=17   claude
  35.5% cpu · n=4    node …bhrain/cli/route
   9.0% cpu · n=62   sh -c
   8.0% cpu · n=1    bash pretooluse.forbid-terms.blocklist.sh
   7.0% cpu · n=1    bash pretooluse.forbid-terms.gerunds.sh
```

**today.** the two hook lines sum to 15%, plus an unquantified share of the 62 `sh -c`. the two
bash lines are the visible part. the wish named them.

## 🔴 .the magnitude claim, questioned

the wish reads that sum as *"15% of a 4-core box"*. **that is very likely 4× too large**, and the
vision repeated it unexamined until review r3.

`ps` reports `%CPU` **normalized to one core** — a single busy thread reads 100%, and a
multithreaded process can exceed it. so `8.0% + 7.0%` is **15% of one core ≈ 3.75% of a four-core
box**.

⚠️ **and `n=1` for each hook corroborates it.** across ~17 clones, exactly **one** instance of each
hook was alive at sample time. these are transient processes, not a sustained draw. a sustained
15%-of-box cost would show many concurrent instances.

⇒ **this does not void the wish.** the fork storm is real (`27` `jq` calls per invocation, read
from source), the wasted parse is real, and the per-edit latency under contention is real. what
changes is **which benefit leads**:

| claim | verdict |
|---|---|
| a clone's **edit latency** falls, most under contention | ✅ the primary, well-evidenced win |
| the fork storm is cut by a large factor | ✅ derivable from source |
| **~15% of a 4-core box is reclaimed** | 🔴 **likely ~3.75%** — raised as **Q8** |

⇒ so the operator's honest expectation is **a modest cpu reclaim and a clear latency win**, never
a large box-level cpu reclaim. a vision that promises the latter sets the operator up to measure
`[t1]`, see a small delta, and conclude the work failed.

**what the beaver could not see from `ps` alone.** those two bash processes are each the **leaf**
of a chain: `sh -c` → `./node_modules/.bin/rhachet run --init …` → the bash hook. the middle link
is the `run.bun.rhachet-run.bc` at the top of their own roll-up. so each of the two hooks costs a
runner boot **plus** its bash forks, and `.claude/settings.json` registers **seven** hooks against
`Write|Edit` — of which these two are the pair the wish may touch.

🔴 **that last clause understated it, and the correction is the whole shape of the fix.** the
wisher took **F7 / lever B** on 2026-09-10 — the wrapper-drop — so `settings.json` now invokes
every hook as `bash <path>`, direct. the count that matters is **14**, never 7: the role registers
14 init hooks in total, and each paid the same wrapper. a `Bash` tool call paid it four times over.

**after.** the beaver re-runs the roll-up on a comparably busy grove. the two bash lines fall to a
fraction of their share, the `sh -c` count falls, and **every** runner boot disappears from the
`run.bun` line — 14 of 14, never the 1-of-7 a merge would have bought.

**the aha.** the leaf forks were the visible cost. the wrapper was the larger one, and it was
already in the beaver's own data, one line above the two they filed against.

## ⚠️ .what the operator must NOT do

read the roll-up once and declare victory. `ps` on a live grove is a sample of a system in motion:
a different clone mix reads as a different number, and a favorable sample proves no claim at all.

⇒ the operator's read is **corroboration**, never the clamp. the clamp is `case=6` — a
deterministic exec tally in ci. acceptance #6 exists precisely because a `ps` roll-up cannot hold
a line over time.
