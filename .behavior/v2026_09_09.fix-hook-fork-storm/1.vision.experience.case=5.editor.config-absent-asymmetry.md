# case=5 — the word lists are absent, and the two hooks fail opposite ways

**actor** editor · **feel** sharp · **care** critipath
**cell** `editor × trips-gerunds × {absent, empty}` (+ the impossible blocklist twin)
**boundary density** rank 3= (B9 B11 = 2–3) — one cause, two opposite outcomes

a cell the grid produced and no free-list would have. the two hooks read two different files, and
when those files go absent the two hooks move in **opposite** directions.

## .the narrative

a fresh clone boots in a repo where the role link has not yet run, so
`.agent/repo=ehmpathy/role=mechanic/inits/claude.hooks/` is bare. both word lists are absent. kai's
clone writes a plain `.ts` file.

**the blocklist hook goes inert.** `blocklist.sh:67-69` finds no file and exits 0. every forbidden
term now lands unremarked. the gate is open and says not one word about it.

**the gerunds hook goes strict.** `gerunds.sh:66-71` guards the file too, but the guard only skips
the *load* — `ALLOWLIST` stays an empty array, and `is_allowed()` then returns false for every
word. the one allowlisted word starts to block. the gate is tighter and stays equally silent.

**one cause. one gate wider, one gate narrower. neither announces it.**

**after.** the same two outcomes, unchanged — the wish's boundary forbids a policy change, and a
degraded-mode change is a policy change. what the fix owes here is narrower and firmer: the cheap
pre-scan must not make the asymmetry *worse*. a pre-scan that consults a cached list will, with no
list at hand, answer for a list that is not there.

**the aha.** "absent config" is not one state. it is two, and they point opposite ways.

## 🔴 .the failhide beneath, on record and not repaired here

`blocklist.sh:72` ends `|| echo "[]"`, so a **malformed** list is treated the same as an absent
one — parse fails, terms become `[]`, the gate silently opens. a typo in a `.jsonc` comma disables
the whole blocklist with no signal at all. that is `rule.forbid.failhide`.

⚠️ it is **extant behavior**, and acceptance #2 plus `.the boundary` forbid us to change it inside
this wish. it is caught as a dream instead (see the yield). to repair it here would smuggle a
policy change into a cost change — the exact move the wish names as unreviewable.

## 🔴 .`[t3]` and `[t6]` are the SAME config state, and opposite verdicts

added at peer review r1, which caught that `trips-gerunds × malformed` carried a verdict no
artifact grounded. one file state, two hooks, two directions:

| the state | the blocklist hook | the gerunds hook |
|---|---|---|
| `malformed` json | **exits 0** — `blocklist.sh:72` ends `\|\| echo "[]"`; zero terms ⇒ no content can trip | 🔴 **exits 2** — `gerunds.sh:69` is `jq … 2>/dev/null … \|\| true`, so `ALLOWLIST` is **empty** and `is_allowed()` (`:74-84`) returns false for *every* word |

⇒ **malformed is not a milder `absent` for the gerunds hook. it is the identical fail-closed path** —
so the word it starts to refuse is `string`, a ubiquitous typescript identifier. that is why this
cell is a **critipath**, at the same grade as `[t1]`, and not the itemized alterpath it carried.

⚠️ **the near-miss worth a name.** the axis paragraph had resolved `absent` for both hooks and
`malformed` for the blocklist only — so the gerunds × malformed cell had a **verdict with no
grounds**, and it read as complete because its neighbours were grounded. ⇒ an ungrounded cell in a
walked product does not look empty; it looks like every other cell.

## 🔴 .`[t5]` clamps the one failure the FIX would author

every other cell in this case describes a hole that exists today. `[t5]` describes one the wish's
headline lever would **create**, and it is the reason the lever needs a guard rail:

| | one term holds a regex metacharacter (a human edits a `.jsonc`) |
|---|---|
| **today** — a grep per term (`blocklist.sh:116-129`) | that grep errors (exit 2), its `if` is false. **the other eight still guard.** a small silent hole |
| 🔴 **after the collapse** — one `\b(a\|b\|c…)\b` | the **only** grep errors ⇒ no match ⇒ **all nine permit, silently** |

⚠️ **do not file this beside `[t3]`.** `[t3]`'s failhide is **inherited**, and the wish's boundary is
precisely why we preserve it. `[t5]`'s would be **ours** — no boundary covers a defect the fix
introduces, and acceptance #2 forbids it outright.

⇒ the remedy is one line, and grep already offers it: **exit 1** is *no match*, **exit 2** is *bad
pattern*. part them, fail loud on 2 (`rule.forbid.failhide`). raised as **Q11**; the axis value it
sits on was absent from the walk until review r5.

## 🔴 .the trap `[t4]` guards, for a merged hook

merge the two hooks and one process now holds two lists whose absence means opposite outcomes. a
single "config absent → exit 0" guard at the top would be correct for the blocklist half and
**wrong** for the gerunds half — it would quietly retire the gerund gate.

⇒ a merged hook keeps **two independent config paths**, exactly as it keeps two nudge clocks
(`case=2`). two of the three merge constraints came from the grid rather than from the wish.

## 🔴 .how it fails safe — it does NOT, and that is a rule collision, not an oversight

`rule.require.experience-coverage` is explicit: *"a sharp critipath shown unhandled (no fail-safe)
is a **blocker**"*. graded against the three properties:

| property | today | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **fast** | the hook exits at once | ✅ |
| **loud** | **silent** — no message, no exit code, no log line, in either direction | 🔴 **fails** |
| **teaches the fix** | there is no output at all to teach from | 🔴 **fails** |

⇒ **this sharp critipath does not fail safe, and this wish may not repair it.**

- the wish's boundary says *"do not change what is blocked"* and *"a behavior change smuggled into
  a cost change is unreviewable"*
- a hook that starts to **announce** a degraded config changes observable behavior; a hook that
  starts to **refuse** on one changes policy outright
- so the two mandates collide: the coverage rule demands a fail-safe, the wish forbids the change
  that would supply one

⚠️ **the resolution is not ours to pick.** it is escalated as **Q5** to the wisher rather than
settled by side effect, because a driver who quietly grades a blocker down has decided a wisher's
question for them.

**what this vision does supply, short of a repair:**

1. the edge is **named, demoed, and verdicted** rather than left undeclared — the shift-left this
   whole practice exists for
2. it is **clamped in both directions** (`[t0]`, `[t1]`), so the degraded modes cannot drift
   silently even while they stay silent
3. the repair is **specified in full** in the dream, ready to land the moment a wisher permits it

⇒ this is deliberate deferral with the cost on record, never a gap papered over.
