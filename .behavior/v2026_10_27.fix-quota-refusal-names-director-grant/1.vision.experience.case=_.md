## .the flags on each director line (F11)

the line lifts what the refusal is about and keeps every other permission as the meter holds it:
push lifts for a push refusal (C4, C14 on rebase and push) or a command that pushes (`set --push`,
rebase); stage lifts for a stage refusal (C17); a plain `set` or `release` in a commit-only tree keeps
`--push block`; an absent or unreadable meter prints `--push <allow|block>` so the director chooses.
