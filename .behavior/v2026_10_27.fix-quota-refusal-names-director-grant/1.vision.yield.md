# vision: a quota refusal asks the sponsor, never a human alone

## the outcome world

### before
a driver under a director spends its last commit use. `git.branch.rebase begin` says "ask your human
to grant more". the driver believes it, halts, and pages for a human. its director held the grant
all along. measured 2026-10-27: the svc-quotes #141 row sat ~25 minutes.

### after
every quota refusal names the sponsor, the one who grants this clone its work, whoever that is:

```
✋ ConstraintError: no commit uses left
   ├─ ask your sponsor to grant more
   └─ rhx git.commit.uses set --quant N --push allow
```

a driver under a director reads "sponsor" and asks its director. a clone a human sponsors asks its
human. the text is one voice for every tree; this repo detects no director.

### the aha
`sponsor` already names the party behind a clone's commits (`git.commit.sponsor`). the refusal
reuses it, so no new term enters this repo, and this repo learns naught about the roles above it.

## the ruled shape (commander, 2026-11-18, PR thread r4614076966)

- **say "ask your sponsor"** — generalize to the extant term; add no `director` term here
- **no director detection, no `git.crew.grant` line** — bhuild consumes ehmpathy and bhrain, so
  ehmpathy can not know about bhuild or the director roles above it
  (`.agent/repo=.this/role=any/briefs/rule.forbid.broken-role-composition-order.[rule].md`)
- **the root cure lives in the director** — directors grant unlimited commits, so a driver rarely
  meets this refusal at all. that change belongs to the director's repo; dispatched upstream

this verdict settles F1 (no detection) and replaces the director render the earlier fulcrums shaped.

## scope

three refusals ask the sponsor (F2, ruled), plus the stage refusal that shares their voice:

| refusal | git.commit.set | git.branch.rebase begin | git.release --mode apply | git.commit.push | git.stage.add |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| "no commit uses left" | yes | yes | yes | — | — |
| "push not allowed" | yes | yes | — | yes (tree) | — |
| "no commit quota set" | yes | yes | yes | — | — |
| "stage not allowed" | — | — | — | — | yes |

also in scope (F3, ruled): this repo's `howto.git-commit-set.[lesson].md` and its `.min`.

out: a block above the tree (C8: global or org switch) and a corrupt quota file (C7: a malfunction)
keep their extant fix.

## evaluation

- goal fit: the refusal names the party who can lift it, in a word every sponsor fits.
- pros: one word swap per site; no cross-repo contract; no detection to fail.
- cons: "sponsor" asks the driver to know who sponsors it. a director-run clone knows its director
  from its own boot; a human-run clone knows its human.
- pit of success: the fix command still prints at every site, so a sponsor who reads the relay
  runs it with no further lookup.
