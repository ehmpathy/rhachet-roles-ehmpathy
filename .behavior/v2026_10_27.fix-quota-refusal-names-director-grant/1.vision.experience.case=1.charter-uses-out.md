# case 1: a director governs the tree, uses out — ask the director

feel: sharp · care: critipath (the incident)

## .narrative

a driver works the svc-quotes #141 tree under its director. it has spent its last commit use, and
`git.branch.rebase begin --mode apply` refuses. the refusal no longer says "ask your human". it says
a director governs this tree, and prints the one call the director runs:

```
🐢 hold up dude...

🐚 git.branch.rebase begin
   └─ error: no commit uses left

a director governs this tree; ask your director to grant more. the director runs:
  $ rhx git.crew.grant --tree svc-quotes.beav.fix-ask-for-details-survives-crash --quota <n> --push allow --why '<why>'
```

the driver cannot run that line; `git.crew.grant` is a director skill. it relays the ask to its
director, not a human. the tree slug is the worktree dir name, as the crew verbs take it, so the
director fills `<n>` and `<why>` and runs it. the quota refills; the driver re-runs the rebase. the
row idles for one round trip, not 25 minutes. no human is paged.

## .bdd sketch

```
given('[case1] a tree a director governs, quota spent (uses = 0, push allow)')
  when('[t0] git.branch.rebase begin --mode apply')
    then('exit code unchanged from main (1 on this surface)')
    then('stdout names "no commit uses left"')
    then('stdout names "ask your director" and "the director runs:"')
    then('stdout names `rhx git.crew.grant --tree svc-quotes.beav.fix-ask-for-details-survives-crash --quota <n> --push allow --why`')
    then('stdout does NOT name "ask your human"')
    then('stdout matches snapshot')
  when('[t1] git.commit.set --mode apply')
    then('exit 2; same director fix, in its print_instruction shape')
  when('[t2] git.release --mode apply')
    then('exit 2; same director fix, in its indented shape')
  when('[t3] the driver relays the printed call to its director, who fills <n> and <why> and runs it')
    then('the call needs no other edit: it carries the real tree slug, basename of the repo root')
    then('the driver re-runs [t0] and the rebase proceeds')  # out of this repo's test scope; the grant lives in uladkasach/nheuron role=director
```

## .the clamp

`[t0]` is the clamp: red on main (it reads "ask your human"), green with the change. revert the
change → red again.
