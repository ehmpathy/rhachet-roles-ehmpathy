# case 7 — `roles cost --subject 101` reads over the cap

sharp · critipath · density 1 (B2) · the command the wish names

why critipath: the wish names this command as its proof of done. a reviewer who runs it and sees ≈
12.3k with no explanation concludes the wish is unmet; there is no other in-band path to the truth.

## narrative

> a reviewer reads the pr, sees "101 = 2,894 ≤ 10k", and runs the wish's command:
> `npx rhachet roles cost --repo ehmpathy --role mechanic --subject 101`. it reads ≈ 12.4k against the
> role's 30k budget. the reviewer opens `boot.yml`; atop `subject.101` a header comment says: rhachet
> 1.50.0 unions `always` into every subject; the 101 slice alone costs 2,894, clamped ≤ 10k in
> `boot.budget.integration.test.ts`; the union lifts with a subject opt-out in rhachet. the
> clamp's own log line prints both numbers side by side. the pr body says the same. the surprise
> lasts one read.

## fail-safe

- **fast** — the pr body leads with the slice number and the union caveat, so a reviewer meets the
  explanation before the command; the clamp's log ([t2]) catches both numbers at the suite boundary.
- **loud** — that log prints the slice beside the 10k cap and, under it, the union with no cap (the
  union is graded against the role's 30k budget).
- **teaches** — the `subject.101` header comment names the cause and the rhachet change that lifts it.
- the bound: `roles cost`'s own output is rhachet's; it cannot be annotated from this repo until the
  rhachet change (F1) lands.
