## the outcome world

a director seat enrolls `mechanic::101`. it boots its own director briefs, the architect overlay, and
a ≈ 2.9k mechanic slice: eight code rules in cue form. every mechanic hook still fires.

a director cures a shared tool in seat. the cure must meet the eight rules — the ones a cure most
often breaks and no hook catches:

- errors: failfast · failloud · no failhide · semantic exit codes
- mutation + types: idempotent mutation · no `as` cast
- repairs: a test per fix · a clamp that goes red without the fix

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| smallest mechanic slice | `subject.arch` ≈ 10.4k (always + 3 domain-object rules), and none of the eight cure rules | `subject.101` ≈ 2.9k, exactly the eight cure rules |
| a director writes a cure | hooks catch syntax, terms, `/tmp`, cross-repo; no say rule names failfast, clamp, or exit codes | same hooks, plus eight cue rules at say |
| context spent on mechanic | full role = 27.8k atop the director's own boot | ≤ 10k cap; measured ≈ 2.9k |

aha: **101 = the hooks, plus the few rules a hook cannot check.** the hooks carry most of mechanic's
value to a non-mechanic seat, at 0 boot tokens; the say list only fills the gap they leave.

## 🔴 the constraint this repo cannot lift alone

rhachet 1.50.0 (latest published) refuses three things the wish assumes:

| the wish assumes | rhachet 1.50.0 does | cite |
|---|---|---|
| 101 boots apart from `always` | absorbs `always` first, unconditionally; no opt-out key or flag | `computeBootPlan.js:203`; `RoleBootSpec.js:75-78` |
| `subject.101` declares its own `budget:` | throws ConstraintError on a budget inside a section | `parseRoleBootYaml.js:31-32` |
| a seat enrolls `mechanic::101` | no `::` focus parser; enroll boots all subjects | `getRoleDeltas.js:34-61`; `setBrainDirBoot.js:29`; rhachet#541 open |

so today `roles cost --subject 101` would read always ∪ 101 ≈ 12.4k, over the 10k cap.

the vision splits the work by owner (fulcrum F1):

- **this repo** — declare `subject.101` (eight say rows, a ref list); clamp its own cost ≤ 10k in a
  test that costs the 101 section alone; leave every other subject and the full role untouched.
- **rhachet** — a subject-level `always` opt-out, a per-subject budget, and the `::` enroll focus.
  inflight in crew `rhachet.beav.feat-enroll-role-subject-focus`; not built here. (rhachet#541,
  per-clone subject choice, is a neighbor.)

when rhachet ships, the clamp moves into boot.yml as `budget:` and `roles cost --subject 101` reads ≈
2.9k direct.

## mental model

- a director's words: "the mechanic hooks plus the error rules." "mechanic lite."
- our words: a subject focus; say vs ref; a budget clamp.
- analogy: a lifeguard cert vs a marine-biology degree. a director in the water needs the whistle (the
  hooks) and the few rescue rules, not the whole syllabus.

## evaluation

- goals: own say list ✅ · hooks intact ✅ · ≤ 10k ✅ for the slice (≈ 2.9k) · ⚠️ "never inherits
  `always`" and "declared `budget:`" wait on rhachet (F1).
- pros: cheap; each row names a cure a seat writes; no other subject moves.
- cons: eight rules now appear in two subjects. a rename breaks both; the extant path test (`have
  every brief path point to a file on disk`) catches it.
- edge: `failloud` has no `.min`; it boots full and still fits (F2).
- edge: `subject.flow` holds `test-covered-repairs` at ref; 101 holds it at ref too, so it cannot
  upgrade the flow ref in the full role (F6, ruled B).
- edge: rhachet de-dups across sections by path, first say wins — so `--subject 101,code.prod` boots
  each rule once.

## what is awkward?

  rhachet allows the latter.
