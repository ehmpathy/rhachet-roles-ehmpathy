## the contract

the rows live in one place each, the `subject.101` section of the role's boot.yml; its header names
what the section is, why, and where its cap lives:

| role | section | say · ref | cap (in `SUBJECT_BUDGETS`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| mechanic | `src/domain.roles/mechanic/boot.yml` | 26 · 6 | 10k |
| architect | `src/domain.roles/architect/boot.yml` | 8 · 9 | 6k |
| ergonomist | `src/domain.roles/ergonomist/boot.yml` | 5 · 6 | 3.5k |

each section declares `inherit: { always: false }`. each say row is say in another section of its
role, which `boot.budget.integration.test.ts` asserts. rhachet boots each say path as its `.min`
where one exists. the clamp costs a manifest with `always` emptied and the section alone, via
`roles cost --what <it> --subject 101`.

## mental model

- a director's words: "the mechanic hooks plus the error rules." "mechanic lite."
- our words: a subject focus; say vs ref; a budget clamp.
- analogy: a lifeguard cert vs a marine-biology degree. a director in the water needs the whistle (the
  hooks) and the few rescue rules, not the whole syllabus.

## evaluation

- goals: own say list ✅ · hooks intact ✅ · ≤ 10k ✅ (seat ≈ 7.9k) · never inherits `always` ✅
  (`inherit: { always: false }`, rhachet 1.51.0) · ⚠️ declared `budget:` refused by rhachet through
  1.53.0; each cap lives in the test, and a per-subject budget is asked of rhachet (S4).
- pros: cheap; each row names a cure a seat writes; no other subject moves.
- cons: eight rules now appear in two subjects. a rename breaks both; the extant path test (`have
  every brief path point to a file on disk`) catches it.
- edge: `failloud` has no `.min`; it boots full and still fits (F2).
- edge: `subject.flow` holds `test-covered-repairs` at ref; 101 holds it at ref too, so it cannot
  upgrade the flow ref in the full role (F6, ruled B).
- edge: rhachet de-dups across sections by path, first say wins — so `--subject 101,code.prod` boots
  each rule once.
