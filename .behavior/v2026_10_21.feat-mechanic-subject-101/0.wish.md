# wish: a `subject.101` in role=mechanic's boot.yml — the basics, ≤ 10k

## the need

director seats will enroll `mechanic::101` (see ehmpathy/rhachet, enroll at a subject focus):
mechanic's hooks plus the core code rules, so a cure a director writes in its own seat still
meets them. today any mechanic subject also boots `always` (~19k), and the smallest subject
(`arch`) costs 22.2k. the full role costs 65.1k.

## what done means

1. `boot.yml` declares `subject.101` with its OWN say list. it never inherits `always`
   (that alone is ~19k, and a 101 on top of it reads ~25k). declared `budget: tokens:` ≤ 10k.
2. every hook of the role stays (hooks follow the role; they cost 0 boot tokens). they are the
   main reason 101 exists: permission check, term checks, cross-repo, tmp, stderr, shell
   syntax, planmode, test-background.
3. the say rows. the bar: a rule an in-seat cure must meet, which no hook already enforces.
   each row with its argument:
   - **failfast** (`rule.require.failfast` code.prod) — a director cures its own tools in seat;
     a tool that limps past a bad state reports a false green to every seat that reads it.
   - **failloud** (`rule.require.failloud` code.prod) — the cure's error is read by another
     clone with no context; ConstraintError vs MalfunctionError tells it to fix input or retry.
   - **forbid failhide** (`rule.forbid.failhide` code.prod, the .min) — the twin of failfast:
     a catch-all in a crew verb hides the one signal a supervisor relies on.
   - **test-covered repairs** (`work.flow/diagnose/rule.require.test-covered-repairs`) — a
     director fixes defects in shared tools; a fix with no test returns on the next edit.
   - **clamp edge cases** (`work.flow/diagnose/rule.require.clamp-edge-cases`) — the clamp must
     go red without the fix. a seat that skips this ships a test that guards naught.
   - **forbid as-cast** (`code.prod/pitofsuccess.typedefs/rule.forbid.as-cast`, the .min) —
     an in-seat ts cure under pressure reaches for `as`; the cast hides the defect from tsc.
   - **forbid nonidempotent mutations**
     (`code.prod/pitofsuccess.procedures/rule.forbid.nonidempotent-mutations`, the .min) —
     crew verbs and hooks re-run on every wake, compact, and retry; a non-idempotent cure
     duplicates its effect each time.
   - **exit-code semantics** (`code.prod/pitofsuccess.errors/rule.require.exit-code-semantics`)
     — hooks and gates act on exit 2 vs 1; a wrong code fails a PreToolUse gate open.
4. out of 101, on purpose: the tone and prose overlays (role=architect, which every director
   boots, carries the same prose overlay), the skill headers (the hooks and `rhx` reach the
   skills), domain-object, glossary, and test-frame rules (crew craft, not in-seat cures).
5. each row at `say` in its cue form (the .min where one exists); arguments and demos at `ref`.
4. `npx rhachet roles cost --repo ehmpathy --role mechanic --subject 101` reads under the budget.
   the pr body carries that number.
5. no change to the full role or to its other subjects.

## why now

the commander's direction, 2026-10-21. it pairs with the boot clamp at
ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy#1028 (`beav/fix-pin-role-boot-budgets`).
