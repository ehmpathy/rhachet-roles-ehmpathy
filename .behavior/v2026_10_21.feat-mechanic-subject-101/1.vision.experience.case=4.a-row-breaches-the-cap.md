# case 4 — a say row pushes 101 past the cap

sharp · critipath · density 1 (B1) · fail-safe spotlight

## narrative

> six months on, a maintainer adds the full `rule.require.what-why-headers` and three demos to
> `subject.101` at say. they run the suites. the boot-budget suite goes red and prints
> `mechanic subject.101: 10,840 / 10,000 tokens` with the hint `on red: trim subject.101 — move a row
> to ref, or condense it to a .min`. they move the demos to ref; it goes green.

## timeline (sketch)

```
given('[case4] subject.101 whose say rows cost past 10_000')
  when('[t0] the boot-budget suite runs')
    then('it fails')                                               # fast — first suite to read it
    then('the failure names subject.101 and prints tokens / cap')  # loud
    then('the failure names the fix: move to ref, or condense')    # teaches
```

## fail-safe

fast: the clamp runs in the integration suite on every push. loud: names the subject and both
numbers. teaches: the hint names the two levers. a cap that only asserts `≤` with no subject name
fails this bar.
