## .how to read the verdicts

- **demoed** — a real experience, demonstrated in a `case=N` file
- **itemized** — a real experience, on record, deliberately not demoed
- **forbidden** — attemptable; the behavior must reject it (an invariant)
- **impossible** — void by nature

care = `freq × cost`: does a workable alternative reach the goal? **critipath** = high care;
**alterpath** = lower care. a care grade is not a risk dial: to move it for prudence changes what the
contract owes, not only what it says.
