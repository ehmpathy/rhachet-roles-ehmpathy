## .the gap, precisely

- the keyrack-not-configured arm — `🥥 to fix this, ask a human to either:`

snapshots (dozens of entries) and in their assertions.

  (`├─ your commit is pushed; to open its pr, either:`), the numbered options follow
- or route both through `print_coconut_hint` so the header is structural
- resnap `git.commit.push` + `git.commit.set`; update the asserts that pin the old header

## .why it is not done in this round

the blocks predate the branch, and the fix ripples through two suites' snapshots and asserts — a
sweep of its own, not a ride-along on a sponsor-source fix.
