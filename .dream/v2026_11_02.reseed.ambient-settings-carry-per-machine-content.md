# 🌙 dream: tracked ambient settings stop carry per-machine content

kind: reseed · owner: ehmpathy/rhachet (the generator of `.agent/.actors/actor.via.repo=ambient/brain/.claude/`) · caught 2026-11-02

## .the cue that fired
the vision for `fix-rebase-sets-aside-actor-drift` asked "what is the root cause of the drift?" and the answer sat in another repo.

## .the gap, precisely
the ambient `.gitignore` tracks `settings.of.grove.json` and `settings.of.repo.json` on purpose. both regenerate with per-machine content:
- `settings.of.grove.json` is a symlink whose target embeds the home dir (`/home/vlad/.claude/settings.json` → `/home/camper/.claude/settings.json`)
- `settings.of.repo.json` embeds home paths in `claudeMdExcludes`, and hook commands that shift with installed role versions

so every clone tree on a different machine, or at a different role version, holds two unstaged edits it must never commit.

## .the shape of the fix
in rhachet: either untrack the per-machine files (generate them, ignore them), or emit machine-neutral content (e.g. a relative or `~`-based target, version-free hook commands). either removes the drift at source.

## .why it is not done in this round
another repo, and a contract change to what the ambient dir tracks. this route fixes the rebase skill, which has standalone value: any unstaged edit, not only ambient drift, blocks a rebase today.
