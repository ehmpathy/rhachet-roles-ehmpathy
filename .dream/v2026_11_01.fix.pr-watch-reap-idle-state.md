# 🌙 dream: reap the pr-watch state a watch or a key leaves behind

kind: fix · owner: repo=ehmpathy role=mechanic (git.pull.watch) · caught 2026-11-01

## .the cue that fired

5.1 i005 r011 nitpick.6: no step reaps state. the ledger half is fixed this round (a set,
guarded by `grep -qxF`); the cache and key-dir halves are not.

## .the gap, precisely

- each pr's `cache/<owner>.<name>.pr=<n>/` dir (etags and bodies) stays after its last watch leaves
- every `v=<key>/` dir stays after its daemon exits; a dev worktree makes one key per edit of
  the daemon files, each with a full `bin/` copy

## .the shape of the fix

- `del_watch_record_locked` removes the pr's cache dir when the last record of that pr goes,
  under the same lock
- `get` (or the boot hook) reaps a key dir with no live daemon, no watch, and no outbox entry,
  under that key's lock; it names each reap in one line
- `daemon.log` gets a cap (5.1 i006 r011 nitpick.10): a fault line per tick adds ~140 KB a
  day for the life of a watch. at boot and once a day, keep the last 1 MB; a fault repeated
  each tick logs once, then once an hour while it lasts

## .why it is not done in this round

a reap of a key dir races a `set` that writes its record before it takes the lock (F3); the
guard wants its own clamp and review, apart from this round's fixes.
