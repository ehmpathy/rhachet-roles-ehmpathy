# 🌙 dream: a transcript wish needs a speaker index

- **kind** = reseed
- **owner** = ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhuild (the behaver's `1.vision` stone template)
- **caught** = 2026-10-23

## .the cue that fired

route `v2026_10_23.fix-arch-fundamentals`: the wish is a pasted chat with an ai assistant, 1280 lines, no turn marks. mid-drive the wisher had to ask *"to distinguish the two speakers in the convo of the wish"*. the vision stone never asked.

## .the gap, precisely

the `1.vision` stone asks for groundwork on external and internal references. it has no step for a wish whose body is a transcript. in a transcript, the wisher's words and the assistant's words carry different weight: the wisher's are intent, the assistant's are hearsay. this route found five ai claims that were overclaims or unreliable (`appendix/wish.speakers.md`, the audit table), and any of them could have become a premise.

## .the shape of the fix

add to the `1.vision` stone template, under groundwork:

> if the wish holds a transcript (a chat, a call, an interview): index each turn by speaker, quote the wisher's turns verbatim, and audit each non-wisher claim the vision leans on (verified · unsourced · overclaim). write the index to `$route/appendix/wish.speakers.md`.

and a self-review prompt to pair with it in `1.vision.guard`: *"did the vision treat a non-wisher voice as a premise?"*

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE: yes. CLEAN: no** — the template lives in `rhachet-roles-bhuild`; a tree adopts only what is scoped to itself.
