# 🌙 dream: rhachet lets a subject boot apart from `always`, under its own budget

kind: reseed · owner: ehmpathy/rhachet · caught 2026-10-21

## .the cue that fired

route `v2026_10_21.feat-mechanic-subject-101` wants `mechanic::101` ≤ 10k with no `always`. rhachet
1.50.0 refuses both halves.

## .the gap, precisely

- `domain.operations/boot/computeBootPlan.js:203` absorbs `always` before any subject, unconditionally.
- `domain.objects/RoleBootSpec.js:75-78` — a subject section holds only `briefs` + `skills`.
- `parseRoleBootYaml.js:31-32` throws on `budget:` inside a section.
- enroll has no `role::subject` focus (`getRoleDeltas.js:34-61`); see rhachet#541.

## .the shape of the fix

- schema: a subject section may declare `always: false` (or `inherit: none`) and `budget: { tokens }`.
- `computeSubjectModePlan`: skip `absorbSection(config.always)` when every selected subject opts out.
- budget: grade a narrowed payload against the subject's own budget where declared, else the top one.
- enroll: parse `role::subject[,subject]` into a subject focus, pass it to `setBrainDirBoot`.
- `roles boot --role 'mechanic::101'` today exits 2 with `tip: did you \`npx rhachet roles link
  --role mechanic::101\` first?` — a tip that cannot help. until `::` parses, name the focus syntax
  as unsupported; once it parses, the tip goes with it.

another repo; this route may not edit rhachet. the mechanic subject ships here with a test-side cap.
