## .the gap, precisely

twelve `pretooluse.forbid-terms.gerunds.sh` hooks stamp twelve paths into one nudge file at once.
under a full parallel jest run, one stamp vanishes. that is a lost update: a writer's
read-modify-write overwrote a peer's record. either the lock does not cover the read, a lock
acquisition times out and the writer proceeds unlocked, or a stale-lock breaker steals a held lock.
the effect in prod: a HARDNUDGE retry that should pass blocks a second time (the record of the
first block is gone), so a human gets one extra interruption.

## .the shape of the fix

- read the lock path in `_hardnudge.lib.sh`: confirm the read, the merge, and the write sit inside
  one held lock, and that a lock timeout fails (exit 2, named) rather than proceeds unlocked
- check the stale-lock breaker: an age threshold below the worst-case hold under load lets a
  waiter break a live lock
- clamp: rerun `[case2]` under synthetic load (e.g. 48 writers, or a `sleep` inside the held
  section via a test seam) until it goes red on `main`, then green with the fix

## .why it is not done in this round

SAFE yes, CLEAN no: a lock-protocol change in a lib every forbid hook sources, with no tie to the
settings-layer fix this route ships. it needs its own red-first clamp under load.
