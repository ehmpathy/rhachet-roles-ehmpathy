the repo-rules lane on the pressViaPipeline route raised `rule.forbid.logic-in-role-dirs` twice against `skills/brief.condense/domain.operations/press/pressViaPipeline.ts`.

## .the gap, precisely

the librarian keeps operations inside its skill dirs:
- `brief.condense/domain.operations/{press,supply,verify}/*`: pressViaPipeline, assertStability, checkRetention, degerund, restoreKernels
- `brief.compress/compress.{via.bhrain,via.llmlingua,shared}.ts`: the compressors and countTokens

## .the shape of the fix

1. move the compressors + `countTokens` to `src/domain.operations/compress/`
2. move the condense ops to `src/domain.operations/condense/{press,supply,verify}/`, tests with them
3. update imports in `brief.compress.ts`, `brief.condense.ts`, the perfeval runners, and the tests
4. run types, the librarian unit and integration suites, and the build (dist/ must still ship each skill)

## .why it is not done in this round
