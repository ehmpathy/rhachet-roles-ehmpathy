## .the shape of the fix

- the settings read: read the committed source of truth (`src/domain.roles/mechanic/inits/init.claude.*`
  or `git show HEAD:<path>`), not the host's linked settings

- pnpm / yarn: fake binaries on `PATH`, as the set.package suites fake npm and curl, so the lock
  regen runs everywhere; or fail fast with a `ConstraintError` that names the install
- gh auth: fail fast with a `ConstraintError` and the keyrack unlock hint
- the CI skips: find what the real-npm cases need that ci lacks, then provide it or move the case
  to a suite ci runs with that resource
- the librarian skips: assert on the stable shape of a brain's output (structure, counts, kernels
  kept) rather than a snapshot of its words; bump test-fns once `.repeatably` reports a SOME pass;
  swap the brain whose safety filter misfires. owner: the librarian role
