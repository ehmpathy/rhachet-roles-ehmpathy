# 🌙 dream: a review lane reports `timed out after 21 minutes` when the brain refused on quota

- **kind** = reseed
- **owner** = ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain
- **caught** = 2026-09-21

## .the cue that fired

nine of eleven peer lanes returned no readable verdict. one round split three ways:

| group | lanes | stderr |
|-------|-------|--------|
| timed out | r1, r3, r4, r7, r8 | `💥 malfunction: review timed out after 21 minutes` |
| died at once | r2, r5, r6, r9 | `💥 failed with an error` — no detail |
| named the cause | r10, r11 | `You've hit your limit · resets 12:10am (UTC)` |

one wall, three reports. the account's brain quota was exhausted.

## .the gap, precisely

- `review timed out after 21 minutes` is a well-formed, untrue report (`rule.forbid.failhide`).
- `💥 failed with an error` names no error (`rule.require.errors-name-the-fix`).
- cost on this route: three rounds of wrong diagnosis. the artifact grew every round, so a size story
  fit every round — a correlate mistaken for a cause. it led to guard-bind probes and a planned
  escalation for budget, and neither could fix a quota wall.

## .the shape of the fix

the refusal text already reaches stdout (r10/r11 prove it); other lanes discard it.

1. classify a quota/limit refusal as `quota`, not `timeout`. surface its text and reset time verbatim.
2. never report a duration for a refusal. fail fast with the cause.
3. make `failed with an error` name the error.
4. consider one round-level quota pre-check: eleven lanes × 21 minutes is ~4 hours to learn one fact.

## .why it is not done in this round

the review harness lives in `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain`. the fix cannot ride this diff.

## .the lesson

a hypothesis that fits every observation is not thereby confirmed. the true report came from the
fastest failure.
