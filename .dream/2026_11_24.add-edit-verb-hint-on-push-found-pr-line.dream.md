## .the gap, precisely

## .the shape of the fix
under the found line, one branch: `to edit it: rhx git.pull.title set · rhx git.pull.description set`. resnap the found-pr cases (`[case18c][t3]`, `[case28]`). precedent: `git.commit.set` names `git.branch.cutover` at its moment of need.
