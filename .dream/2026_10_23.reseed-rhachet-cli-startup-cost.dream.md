# 🌙 dream: rhachet's `roles boot` / `roles cost` start in seconds, not ~20s

kind: reseed · owner: ehmpathy/rhachet · caught 2026-10-23

## .the cue that fired

after the swap to rhachet 1.50.1, the full suite in rhachet-roles-ehmpathy went red on 7 spawn
timeouts, no assertion failed. `npx rhachet roles boot --role mechanic --subject nonexistent`,
which only has to reject a subject name, took 22,249 ms in jest.

## .the gap, precisely

- `boot.yml.integration.test.ts` `[t2]` (fail fast on an unknown subject): 22,249 ms
- `--subject code.test` boot: 30,247 ms, timed out at 30s
- `boot.budget` isolation costs: 40-74 s per pair
- log: `.log/role=mechanic/skill=git.repo.test/what=integration/2026-10-23T00-26-07Z.stderr.log`

## .the shape of the fix

profile `rhachet roles boot` cold start: the tokenizer load (o200k_base) and the role/repo walk
likely run before argv is checked. validate argv and the subject name first, load the tokenizer
lazily (only `cost` and the stats block need it), and cache the role walk per process.

## .why it is not done in this round

another repo; this tree may not edit rhachet. here the spawn clocks were raised to 120s (F19).
