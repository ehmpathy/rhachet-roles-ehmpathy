## .the shape of the fix

six rules for the librarian:

| rule | says |
|---|---|
| `rule.require.define-before-inventory` | define the concept in its own terms, from more than one source, before you list its members. an entry joins by the definition, never because it shares a source's list |
| `rule.require.ours-first` | when the subject is knowledge we already practice, census our own briefs and code first. external canon backs our entries and fills gaps |
| `rule.forbid.single-source-census` | an inventory of an open concept drawn from one author or era is a catalog of that source (`catalog.of=abstraction.from=dijkstra`); it may not claim the concept |
| `rule.require.declare-each-source-lens` | each source carries the question it answered. a source's lens may not become the concept's definition |
| `rule.always.discover-on-blocked-term` | a hook or rule that blocks a word is a cue to find the domain's own word, never to grab the nearest synonym |
| `rule.require.vet-example-nouns` | every noun in a brief's example passes the ubiqlang test (one sense, the domain's own word), since every clone copies it. vet it; `domain.terms/` holds only the repo's own vocabulary |

per `rule.require.forbid-pole-on-boot-choice`, the forbid boots as say with the cure; its require twins boot as ref.
