## .the gap, precisely

the case guards one invariant: a hook past its deadline never waits on the nudge lock (a wait costs ≈ 2s of sleeps, `ATTEMPT_LOCK_LIVE_TRIES × 0.05s`). the bound measures that wait plus hook startup, and startup grows with host load. the line is on `origin/main` as written.

## .the shape of the fix

measure a no-lock run of the same aged hook copy first, then bound the locked run's excess over it: `expect(Date.now() - startedAt - baselineElapsed).toBeLessThan(1000)`, with a `.proof` comment that names the invariant. proven in that route: a mutant that waits out the lock before its deadline check → red (`Expected: < 1000`); restored → green; file 217/217. a peer lane asked that the relative form carry a human verdict, so it goes to main with its own review.

an alternative that drops time entirely: have the hook print a distinct line when it skips the wait, and assert on that line.
