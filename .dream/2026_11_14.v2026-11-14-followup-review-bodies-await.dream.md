# 🌙 dream: review summary bodies can await a reply too

- kind: followup
- owner: the #1171 tree (git.release gate) or a later git.pull.comment tree
- caught 2026-11-14, by .behavior/v2026_11_14.feat-pull-comment-hints-unanswered

## .the cue that fired
a self review of the vision asked "do two sources cover all comments?". the answer is no.

## .the gap, precisely
the unanswered read reads `issues/{n}/comments` and `pulls/{n}/comments`. a review submission's own body (`GET repos/{o}/{r}/pulls/{n}/reviews`, `.body`) is a third source. #693's approval body was one of them ("approved, given the comments left are addressed first"). live on ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy#1022: review bodies `""`, `"cool"` (APPROVED). most are noise, but a changes-requested body can hold a real question.

## .the shape of the fix
read `pulls/{n}/reviews` in the shared unanswered operation. count a non-empty body with state `CHANGES_REQUESTED` or `COMMENTED`, by a human who is not the pr author, as a top-level wait. skip `APPROVED`, or treat a conditional approval as a wait. a top-level `--key` answer (no reply endpoint) clears it.

## .why it is not done in this round
the wish bounds this tree to the hint, and names "review comment" rows. which review states count is a policy call for the release gate (#1171), whose wish says "every review comment thread and every issue comment".
