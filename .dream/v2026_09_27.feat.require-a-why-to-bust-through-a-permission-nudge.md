## .the gap, precisely

- today a bare retry of the exact command inside the window busts through to a human prompt
- the nudge makes the paved path visible, but the retry needs no justification
- ⇒ a bot can retry out of habit, and each habit retry spends a human's attention

## .the shape of the fix

1. a retry passes to the human only if it carries a `--why` the hook can read — e.g. a shell
   comment at the end of the command, `# --why: <reason>`, which bash ignores and the hook parses
2. a bare retry stays blocked, and the render says how to add a `--why`
3. the `--why` text rides into the `ask` reason, so the human reads it in the prompt, and into the
   ledger row (`why` field)
