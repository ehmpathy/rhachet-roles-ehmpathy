# 🌙 dream: `keyrack get --json` names when the grant dies

- kind: reseed
- owner: rhachet (keyrack)
- caught 2026-11-21

## .the cue that fired

the pr-watch daemon fix wanted to hold a token until shortly before it dies. the type allows it (`KeyrackKeyGrant.expiresAt`), and keyrack's own cache knows it, but `get` drops it.

## .the gap, precisely

`getKeyrackKeyGrant.js:53`–`60` (rhachet 1.54.0) rebuilds the grant from the daemon's key entry with `slug`, `key`, `reach`, `source`, `env`, `org`, and no `expiresAt`. the daemon's entry holds one (`vaultAdapterOsDaemon.js:79`–`81`; `computeExpiresAt.js` clamps it to the unlock lapse and the mech's own). so a caller of `get --json` cannot learn how long its token lives, and must spend a 401 to find out, or poll keyrack on a guess.

## .the shape of the fix

carry `expiresAt: keyEntry.expiresAt` onto the rebuilt grant. `--json` then emits it with no further change, and `status` and `get` agree on a key's life.

## .why it is not done in this round

another repo's code. the pr-watch daemon works without it (it holds until a 401); with it, the daemon can drop a token at keyrack's lapse at zero extra cost (F1 option c).
