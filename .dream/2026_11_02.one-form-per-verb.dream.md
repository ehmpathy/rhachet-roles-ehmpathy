# 🌙 dream: one form per verb in every git.pull.* output

kind: followup · owner: ehmpathy/mechanic · caught 2026-11-02

## .the cue that fired

5.3 self review (has-ergonomics-validated) on `feat-git-pull-title-description`: a real plan on
#1173 ended `rhx git.pull.description.set --auth as-human --from … --mode apply`, while the get hint
one step earlier read `rhx git.pull.description set --pr 1173 …`.

## .the gap, precisely

refusals and plan hints come from `as_comment_cmd_in_mode` and `rhx $SKILL …` strings, which print
the dotted form. help examples, the `get --to` hint, `commands.alternatives.jsonc`, and the comment
family's `git.pull.comment <verb>` dispatch teach the spaced form. both dispatch; a reader meets two.

## .the shape of the fix

pick spaced (the dispatcher form the help already teaches). have `as_comment_cmd_in_mode` print
`rhx git.pull.<noun> <verb>`; replace `rhx $SKILL` in refusals with one `as_pull_cmd` read; resnap
both families; one test that greps every snapshot for a dotted `rhx git.pull.<noun>.<verb>` and
expects zero.

## .why it is not done in this round

safe, not clean: the shared builder and ~40 refusal strings across ten verbs and two families,
plus their snapshots. fulcrum F20 records the call.
