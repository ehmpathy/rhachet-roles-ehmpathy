# 🌙 dream: review and route records mask their log-path clock stamps

- kind: reseed (bhrain)
- owner: ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain — the reviewer (`rhx review`) and the route engine
- caught 2026-11-22

## .the cue that fired

a 5.3 peer lane found that the declapract husky practice (`check.timestamps.sh`) forbids a dash clock in any staged non-`.ts`/`.sh` file. every reviewer given and report this route holds names its log dir (`.log/bhrain/review/<date>T<hh>-<mm>-<ss>-<ms>Z.pid…/`), and `.route/approvals.jsonl` holds stamps too. so a repo on that practice cannot commit its route's review trail.

## .the gap, precisely

the tool emits a clock into an artifact it expects to be tracked; the practice forbids a clock in any tracked file. the two contracts disagree, and the route's human cannot reconcile them by hand (`.route/` is sealed; a given is a peer's record).

## .the shape of the fix

in bhrain: render log paths in givens and reports relative and stamp-free (e.g. the review's hash or ordinal, `…/review/i001.r003/`), and store approval times as dates, or as a hash-ordered sequence, not clocks. clamp: a given written by a test run passes `check.timestamps.sh`.

## .why it is not done in this round

another repo's tool; this route leaves the records out of its commit (fulcrum F11).
