## .the gap, precisely

- **position predicates are scattered.** `phase × halt × from_kind` is read at five sites:
  `is_park_expected`, `guard_head_at_cutover_position`, abort's undo branch, abort's quota line,
  and the report. each site hand-derives what a phase implies (park made? cut made? unit spent?)
- **the pid-lock primitive lives in the meter's file.** `git.branch.uses.operations.sh` holds about
  150 lines of pid lock (`set_pid_lock`, `is_pid_lock_held_by_self`, the exit-release registry);
  the cutover worktree lock imports it from there. a third consumer pays for that home
- **the stage-grant read has four by-path copies**: `git.commit.set`, `git.stage.add`,
  `git.commit.uses.local`, and the cutover halt render (`get_halt_stage_block`). the halt mirrors
  stage.add's two gates (global blocker, then `.stage`) in the same order, by hand
