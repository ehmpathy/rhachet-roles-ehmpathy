## .the gap, precisely
- `git.commit.uses --global block` writes `$HOME/.rhachet/storage/repo=ehmpathy/role=mechanic/.meter/git.commit.uses.jsonc`
  (`git.commit.operations.sh:41-42`, `git.commit.uses.global.sh:140-145`); set, push, and stage

  (`git.release.sh:264-268`), which no verb writes
- so a human's global block does not stop a release: it falls to the quota guard, which asks for a
  grant (in a director tree, the director's) that cannot lift a block

3. a clamp in the release suite: `git.commit.uses --global block`, then `git.release --mode apply`
