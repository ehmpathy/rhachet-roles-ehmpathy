## .the cue that fired
peer review r011 (enroll-impl-arch-defects), i013, nitpicks 3 and 5, on route `.behavior/v2026_10_26.fix-release-blocked-on-unanswered-comments/`.

## .the gap, precisely
- **tree glyphs threaded by hand.** `emit_transport_status` (`git.release._.emit_transport_status.sh`) passes "is this the last branch?" state down the ladder: `rebase_automerge_is_last`, a `comment_refusal` parameter on `_emit_check_failure_hints`, and a `print_hint_branch` twin of `print_errors_hint`. each new line forces every earlier line to know what follows it.
- **a hand-spelled indent.** `_watch_pr_comment_gate` (`git.release._.emit_transport_watch.sh`) spells `automerge disabled [removed]` itself, since `print_automerge_status` hardcodes a 3-space prefix.
- **exit 2 means two things.** a failed check is retryable under `--retry`; a comment refusal is terminal. `_exit_on_comment_refusal` keeps them apart with an `exit 2` three frames down, and `git.release.sh` reads `status_result` in three separate ladders.

## .the shape of the fix
1. split `emit_transport_status` into a pure outcome transformer, `asTransportOutcome` → `clear | rebase | checks-failed | refused`, plus a render that collects its branches and marks the last one itself
2. give `print_automerge_status` an `indent` parameter, as `print_comment_status` has; drop the hand-spelled line in the watch
3. name the outcome class once (`retryable | terminal`) and let `git.release.sh` branch on that word, not on the code; retire the deep `exit 2`
4. clamp: the extant p1–p9 snapshots must hold byte for byte

## .why it is not done in this round
SAFE, but not CLEAN: it reshapes the status ladder that every p1–p9 snapshot pins, and the three `status_result` ladders in `git.release.sh`. that is a diff of its own, far past the comment gate this route delivers. the gate today is sound (r011: "no harm I can name").
