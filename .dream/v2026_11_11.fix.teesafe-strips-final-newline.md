# 🌙 dream: teesafe keeps a file's final newline

kind: fix · owner: ehmpathy/mechanic (`claude.tools/teesafe.sh`) · caught 2026-11-11

## .the cue that fired

a restore of two snapshot files from HEAD via `git show HEAD:<path> | rhx teesafe --into <path> --idem upsert`
left a one-line diff in each: `\ No newline at end of file`. a follow-up
`printf '\n\n' | rhx teesafe --idem append` did not add it back.

## .the gap, precisely

teesafe drops the final newline(s) of its stdin (both `upsert` and `append`). a byte-exact pipe
through it is impossible, so it cannot restore a file, and `git restore` is unpaved.

evidence: `git diff --stat` on
`src/domain.roles/mechanic/skills/claude.tools/__snapshots__/grepsafe.integration.test.ts.snap` and
`src/domain.roles/mechanic/skills/git.repo.test/__snapshots__/git.repo.test.play.integration.test.ts.snap`
shows `1 insertion(+), 1 deletion(-)` each; the only change is the absent final newline.

## .the shape of the fix

read stdin byte-exact (`cat > "$tmp"`, never `$(cat)`, which strips final newlines), then move or
append the temp file. clamp: a test that pipes `'a\n'` and asserts the file is exactly 2 bytes; red on
the extant skill.

## .why it is not done in this round

the skill is outside this pr's scope (git.package.patch), and its fix needs its own test and snap.
the two dirty snapshot files are not staged in this pr.
